DATA SOVEREIGNTY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

OUR PROMISE: We respect your privacy. We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data. We do not use third-party ad tracking pixels, cross-site trackers, or predatory surveillance networks.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to deliver educational guides, coaching calls, and member services:

  • Voluntary Form Submissions: When you download our free guide ("Create the Perfect Workout") or submit a contact inquiry, we collect your first name and email address.
  • Membership Registration: When you join the Being of Earth Academy, our secure member portal engine (GoHighLevel) and payment gateway (Stripe) collect your name, billing address, and payment information. Your credit card details are encrypted and processed directly by Stripe; Being of Earth never stores or views your raw credit card numbers.
  • Direct Communications: If you reach out via WhatsApp or email, we retain your communication history solely to provide personal coaching support.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Delivering requested guides, video routines, and course materials.
  • Notifying you of weekly live Q&A call schedules and new educational modules.
  • Managing active subscription billing and processing one-click cancellations.
  • Responding to your direct questions and support inquiries.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Tracking

Being of Earth operates on a strict sovereignty standard. Our static edge website (hosted on Cloudflare Pages) does not load Google AdSense, Meta advertising pixels, TikTok trackers, or invasive data brokers.

4. Data Storage & Security

Member records and billing transactions are secured using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption, 256-bit SSL, and SOC-2 compliant database infrastructure through GoHighLevel and Stripe.

5. Your Rights & One-Click Unsubscribe

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal information at any time. Every automated email we send contains a visible, 1-click unsubscribe link. To request complete deletion of your records, simply email info@beingofearth.com.
