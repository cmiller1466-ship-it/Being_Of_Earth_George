THE PROBLEM

Why Most Health Programs Fall Short

The problem isn't a lack of information. It's knowing what is right for your unique body, lifestyle, and nervous system.

One Size Doesn't Fit All

Generic workout templates and rigid diet plans overlook individual differences in joint anatomy, stress thresholds, and digestive capacity. What works wonders for a 22-year-old fitness influencer often creates joint inflammation and exhaustion for a busy adult.

Treating Symptoms Instead of Root Causes

We often try to treat problems in isolation—taking a pill for digestive distress, stretching a tight hamstring, or meditating for stress. Yet your movement, gut health, beliefs, and sleep are biologically interconnected. Treating parts in isolation rarely produces lasting relief.

Depending on Others for Answers

Therapists and trainers can provide valuable guidance, but real, sustainable progress comes from learning to read your own body. When you understand your own movement compensations and energy triggers, you become empowered to coach yourself for life.

My Goal Is to Help You Become Your Own Best Health Coach

After more than 20 years of working with people to improve their health and physical performance, I’ve learned that lasting progress requires far more than blindly following an aggressive workout routine or restrictive meal plan.

Early in my career as a personal trainer, I watched countless dedicated people push through intense gym programs—only to end up battered with chronic joint pain, disc issues, and exhaustion. That frustration drove me to leave mainstream fitness behind and spend years studying kinesiology, joint decompression, myofascial release, and somatic movement.

I created Being of Earth to bring these essential pieces together in one simple, educational system. My mission is to help you better understand yourself, develop the confidence to make informed decisions, and take sovereign ownership of your health and vitality.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Being of Earth Academy, the Blueprint, and your membership.

How is Being of Earth different from a conventional fitness or wellness app?

Most fitness apps offer static workout templates or generic meal logs without teaching you why your body responds the way it does. Being of Earth is an educational system. We guide you through self-assessments to understand your own body, movement, mindset, and recovery—empowering you to make informed decisions and coach yourself for life.

What is the difference between the Movement Fundamentals Workbook and the Full Academy?

The Movement Fundamentals Workbook is a free 40-page interactive guide introducing George's 5-phase movement framework, daily check-ins, and 7-day experiment. The Being of Earth Academy is our comprehensive digital university and membership. It contains full video courses (Assess & Stretch, Master Your Core, Master Your Mind), weekly live coaching calls with George, and our private member community.

I have recurring joint stiffness or past injuries. Is this safe for me?

Yes. In fact, Being of Earth was built specifically for adults whose bodies have felt battered by high-impact gym routines. George’s background in kinesiology emphasizes joint decompression, antagonist muscle balance, and rotational stability. You will learn how to test your mobility first and work within your body's safe thresholds.

How much daily time commitment does this require?

The curriculum is designed for busy adults. Most daily routines and educational modules take between 15 to 25 minutes. Weekly live coaching calls are recorded and archived inside the portal if you cannot attend live.

How does the 14-day free trial work, and how do I cancel?

When you enroll, you receive immediate 100% free access to all courses, assessments, live coaching, and the community for 14 days. If it's not the right fit, you can cancel inside your member profile with a single click before day 14 and you will never be charged.

Do I need special gym equipment or a fitness facility?

No special gym equipment is required. The Being of Earth curriculum emphasizes bodyweight movement mechanics, joint mobility, floor-based work, and accessible everyday tools (such as resistance bands, a lacrosse ball, or a yoga mat). You can practice the routines comfortably in your living room, hotel room, or outside in nature.
